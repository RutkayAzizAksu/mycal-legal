Frequently asked questions

What is MyCal?

MyCal is an app for tracking your daily calories and nutrition. You can log a meal by taking a photo, saying it out loud, scanning a barcode or searching by hand. Water, steps, weight, cycle tracking and working with a coach are part of the app too.

How does photo analysis work?

Your photo is sent to an AI model, and the name, portion, calories and macros of the meal come back in a few seconds. You can correct the numbers before saving, and your corrections steer later estimates. One analysis a day is free; MyCal Plus makes it unlimited. Results are estimates, not medical advice.

What does MyCal Plus include?

Unlimited photo analysis, menu and nutrition-label scanning, unlimited voice logging, and an AI sports coach that knows your diary, goals and weight.

How do I cancel, and where is my invoice?

The subscription runs through your Apple account. You can cancel at any time on your iPhone under Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Plus stays on until the end of the period. Apple is the seller, so Apple sends the invoice or receipt and handles refund requests (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Is my data safe?

Your entries are sent over an encrypted connection and stored on servers in the European Union. Your data is not sold or shared for advertising. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete Account to permanently delete your account and all your data. This cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple account.

How do I change the app language?

Under Settings > Language you can choose Turkish, English, German, French, Japanese or Korean.

How do I stop the e-mails?

You can turn off the weekly newsletter in Settings or with the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of the e-mail. E-mails about your account (address confirmation, password reset, purchase confirmation) are still sent.
